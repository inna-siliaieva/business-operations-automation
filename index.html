Real Business Examples

See how everyday manual work can become an automated process

Practical examples of common office and business processes that can be simplified, connected, and automated.

Invoice Processing & Payment Tracking

Before

Invoices arrive by email, are checked manually, entered into spreadsheets, sent for approval, and tracked by hand.

Automation

Email → Data Extraction → Validation → Duplicate Check → Approval → Payment Tracking → Reminder

Result

Fewer manual steps, fewer duplicate records, better payment control, and a clearer approval process.

Document Management

Before

Employees download files, rename them manually, decide where they belong, and update document registers by hand.

Automation

Email → Document Recognition → Classification → Rename → Correct Folder → Register

Result

Documents are organized consistently, easier to find, and less dependent on manual filing.

Lead Intake & CRM

Before

New inquiries are copied manually from forms or emails into a CRM, and duplicate contacts can easily be created.

Automation

Lead Form → Contact Check → Create or Update Contact → Create Deal → Associate Records

Result

Faster lead handling, cleaner CRM data, fewer duplicates, and less manual administration.

Daily Operational Reporting

Before

Employees send updates by message, email, or spreadsheet, and a manager must review everything manually.

Automation

Report Form → Google Sheets → Issue Detection → Manager Notification

Result

Consistent reporting, faster issue detection, and less time spent collecting updates.

Budget & Cost Monitoring

Before

Project budgets and actual costs are compared manually, often across several spreadsheets.

Automation

Budget Data + Cost Data → Calculation → Variance Analysis → Risk Status → Alert

Result

Earlier visibility of cost overruns, clearer financial control, and more reliable management information.

Automated Management Reporting

Before

Data from several sources is copied, cleaned, summarized, and formatted manually before a report is sent.

Automation

Data Sources → Merge → Clean → Calculate → Summary → Report Distribution

Result

Faster recurring reports, fewer manual errors, and more consistent management information.
